The Digital Forensics Association is a student-run club at Champlain College for anyone curious about digital forensics, incident response, and the investigative side of cybersecurity. You don't need to be a DFIR major or have any experience to come.

## What we do

- **Weekly meetings.** A member or guest presents a topic, usually followed by a hands-on lab.
- **CTF practice.** We work through forensics challenges together and compete as a team.
- **Guest speakers.** Practitioners from industry and law enforcement share how the work looks day to day.
